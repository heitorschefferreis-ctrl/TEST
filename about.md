# OmniPathfinder AI

**OmniPathfinder AI** is a fully automated decision-making autopilot mod for Geometry Dash 2.2 built with high-efficiency sensor scanning capabilities.

## Dynamic Features
* **Hazard Sensor (Spikes & Blocks):** Scans the physics layout ahead to compute obstacle density and initiate frame-perfect jumps.
* **Interactive Trigger Radar:** Automatically targets and activates Orbs and manages Dash Orb hold timelines correctly.
* **Smart Fly-Mode Vectoring:** Dynamically traces safety center lanes inside Ship, Wave, UFO, and Swing modes, adapting seamlessly to normal or inverted gravity layouts.
* **Visual HUD Radar Overlay:** Renders real-time color-coded visual nodes over hazards (red), triggers (green), and safety paths (yellow).

## Configuration Settings
* **Autopilot Enabled:** Turn the entire system on or off.
* **Draw Radar Overlay:** Toggles debug geometric lines and radar spheres.
* **Lookahead Distance:** How far ahead the bot scans (higher coordinates allow earlier decision setups for faster gamemodes).
